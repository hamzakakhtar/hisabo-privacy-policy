Hisabo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Hisabo ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Hisabo collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Hisabo expense tracking application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Hisabo may collect or process the following information:

  • Email address
  • User account ID
  • Name, if provided by the user
  • Expense and transaction information
  • Group expense information
  • Categories and expense descriptions
  • Photos or receipt images attached to expenses
  • Purchase or subscription information related to premium features
  • Advertising-related identifiers through third-party advertising services

2. Expense and Financial Information

Hisabo allows users to record personal and group expenses, transactions, amounts, balances, categories and related information.

This information is primarily used to provide the expense tracking functionality of the application.

3. Local Storage

Hisabo is designed to support offline expense tracking. Some information may be stored locally on your device so that the application can function without an internet connection.

4. Account and Cloud Sync

Account creation and cloud synchronization may be optional. If you choose to create an account or enable cloud-related features, certain information may be transmitted to our cloud service providers to provide authentication, synchronization and storage functionality.

Hisabo may use Firebase services provided by Google for authentication, database storage and related cloud functionality.

5. Photos and Receipts

Hisabo may allow you to attach photos or receipt images to expenses.

Photos are used to provide expense and receipt management functionality. If cloud synchronization is enabled, applicable photos may be uploaded to cloud storage.

6. Advertising

Hisabo may display advertisements using third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may process certain device or advertising identifiers and other information according to their own privacy policies.

Advertising preferences and personalized advertising may be controlled according to the options available on your device and within applicable Google services.

7. In-App Purchases

Hisabo may offer premium features through in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Payments are processed through the applicable app store, such as Google Play. Hisabo does not receive or store your complete credit card or payment card number through the app store payment process.

8. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Hisabo features
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Synchronize user data when cloud sync is enabled
  • Store and display expenses and transactions
  • Provide group expense functionality
  • Provide premium features
  • Improve application security and reliability
  • Display advertisements where applicable

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the application, such as cloud infrastructure, authentication, analytics or advertising providers.

Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the applicable services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.
